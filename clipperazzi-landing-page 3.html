WHAT IS CLIPPING
TikTok · Instagram Reels · YouTube Shorts

Clipping, Explained.

Clipping turns one piece of long-form content into hundreds of short videos posted by many different creators, so your brand shows up on feeds you could never reach with a single account.

Book a Call →How campaigns work
THE SIMPLE VERSION

One source. Many clippers. Every feed.

A clip is a short, scroll-stopping cut taken from your podcast, video, launch or brand material. Clippers create and post them from their own accounts, and you pay for the views that actually land.

01

You provide the source

Long videos, podcasts, streams or a brand kit, plus clear guidelines on what is on and off brand.

02

Clippers create

Creators cut, caption and publish short videos across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

03

Views get verified

Submissions are reviewed and performance is tracked before any payout goes out.

CLIPPING VS META ADS

Not a replacement. A different engine.

Ads rent attention while the budget runs. Clipping builds attention that comes from real people's pages.
